My Life Story (the short version)

I first touched a computer in 1984 and I have never been the same since. I started out on a Commodore 64, moved up to a 386 with a turbo button, and now I am on a Pentium surfing the Information Superhighway at a blistering 28.8 kbps.

These days I am learning JavaScript (nee LiveScript) and Cascading Style Sheets. Soon I will learn Java and put an applet on this page. Watch this space!
